PHNX Browser
Profiles. Privacy. Control.

Built around
your browsing.

PHNX keeps the everyday browser surface simple while putting profiles, permissions, network choices, and resource behavior within reach.

Isolated profiles

Separate supported cookies, storage, permissions, history, sessions, identity, and network configuration.

Privacy controls

Profile-scoped JavaScript, cookie, pop-up, Safe Browsing, permission, clear-data, and blocker controls.

Resource-aware

Profiles can move through active, idle, frozen, suspended, and recreated lifecycle states.

Baseline blocking

Intercept supported advertising and tracking requests locally, with site exceptions and accurate request counts.

Network choice

Direct, configured proxy, or explicitly selected public proxy routes with clear limitations and warnings.

Browser basics

Tabs, private-tab hygiene, bookmarks, history, downloads, search engines, shortcuts, and full-screen WebView.

Privacy, without theater

Protection and limits, clearly stated.

  • Browser data is stored locally by the app and Android WebView.
  • Public proxies are third-party routes, not anonymity tools.
  • Private tabs provide history and session hygiene, not a separate WebView cookie partition.
  • Android System WebView limits DNS, WebRTC, website notifications, and identity controls.
